CREATOR BIO

Based in Christchurch, NZ. I'm currently navigating working/studying as a fabrication apprentice. Growing up I was always interested in pulling things apart, and on occasion putting them back together.

Now, I build combat robots, play Magic: The Gathering, and spend way too much time accidentally breaking my linux installation. If I'm not striking arcs, I'm plotting what my next project will be.

FROG-ISH

The 30lb undercutter. Made of G450 steel and some bits I had laying around. Designed in FreeCAD, built to take wheels off in the arena. Currently has an 0-2 record, having lost against two gnarly bots, Synthesis30 from the USA, and then Mjolnir; a local bot, while competing under Team Australia at the Robot Rampage world cup event.

JANK CNC

After retiring my first 3D printer, the glorious fire hazard; Anet A8, I decided to use its components to put together a much sturdier 3-axis system around a Ryobi trim router. Linear rails, welded steel frame, and some aluminium components are featured all over to bring the whole thing together.

INEVITABLE END

My first active weapon combat robot, built for the beetleweight (3lb) class. Weapon disk started as an offcut of 10mm mild steel, carved with an angle grinder and balanced with a drill. Now features a high power hub motor, a massive AR600 blade, two huge Lego wheels, and TPU chassis. It does well locally ;)
